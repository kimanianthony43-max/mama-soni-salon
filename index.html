GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know before you come.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to book? ▾

No. Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking on WhatsApp helps us reserve your chair and reduce waiting.

How long do braids take? ▾

Box Braids: 3-5 hrs • Stitch Lines: 2-3 hrs • Knotless: 3-5 hrs • Cornrows: 1-2 hrs • Dreadlocks Retouch: 1-3 hrs. We will confirm your time on WhatsApp.

How do I pay? ▾

Pay in the salon after your service. Exact prices are confirmed before we start — no surprises.

Do you wash hair? ▾

Yes, please ask on WhatsApp when you book so we plan enough time for you.

What should I bring? ▾

Just yourself and your style photo if you have one. We have the rest.
